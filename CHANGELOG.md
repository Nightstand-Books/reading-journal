# Nightstand changelog

Every update to Nightstand is listed here, newest first. The version number shows at the bottom of the account menu in the app.

## How version numbers work

Versions look like **1.4.2** (major.minor.patch):

- **Patch** (1.4.**2**): bug fixes and small tweaks, like a color adjustment or a typo.
- **Minor** (1.**4**.0): new features or noticeable changes, like a new tab or a new setting.
- **Major** (**1**.0.0): big changes to how the app works or how your data is stored.

When a number goes up, the numbers after it reset to 0 (1.4.2 → 1.5.0).

## 1.0.0 (October 8, 2026)

The first numbered release. Earlier builds used date-based versions (for example 2026.10.08-za). This release includes everything below.

### Library and books
- Add books by searching Hardcover, Google Books and Open Library, or enter them by hand.
- Book details include status, format (print, ebook, audiobook), rating, pages, dates, cost, source, review, notes, a favorite quote, a summary, genres, moods and series.
- Community ratings from Hardcover or Open Library, plus Goodreads ratings from imports.
- **Find covers & details** fills in missing covers, descriptions, genres and audiobook lengths for your whole library in one go.
- **Switch cover** lets you pick a different edition's cover or upload a photo of your own copy.
- Library search, plus filters for status, genre, TBR shelf and challenge.
- When **Finished** is selected, a year dropdown filters by All years, This year, a specific year, or No finish date.
- Start and finish dates can be **Exact** (a full date) or an **Estimate** (month and year, or just the year).
- When a book is marked finished, **Pages read** fills in with the full page count.
- **Done** sits in the bottom right corner of a book's page. Deleting a book is a small link that asks you to confirm.
- The edit screen no longer closes when a text selection drag ends outside the box, and it asks before closing if you have unsaved changes.

### Genres and statuses
- The genre menu is in A–Z order and includes Romantasy, Middle grade, Young adult and Children's.
- Pick **Something else…** to type your own genre. It's added to the menu in alphabetical order for next time.
- **Genres & statuses** (account menu) lets you add, rename and remove genres. Renaming a genre also renames it on your books.
- Rename the built-in statuses, or add your own (like Paused or Owned).

### Audiobooks
- Track listening progress in hours and minutes instead of pages.
- Audiobook lengths come from Hardcover. **Find on Hardcover** / **Change edition** lets you pick between editions, like a regular and a dramatized version.
- Finished audiobooks fill in their hours listened automatically.
- **Hours listened** stat tile on the dashboard.

### Dashboard
- Reading goal ring, stat tiles, books finished by month, genres and formats, moods and pace.
- **On the nightstand**: drag books into any order (press and hold on a phone). Hovering highlights a book so it's clear it can be moved.
- **Up next**: choose which want-to-read books appear, and reorder them.
- **Customize dashboard** hides any section or stat tile, and the rest closes up neatly.

### Bookcase
- Each year's finished books sit on a bookcase, with spine colors matched to their covers.
- Spine widths follow page counts.
- **Edit spine**: color, text color, font, height and width.
- **Edit shelves**: add shelves, leave gaps, rearrange books, and add decor and bookends.

### TBR Shelves
- A **TBR Shelves** tab for named lists like "Summer TBR" or "For when I need a good cry."
- A book can be on as many shelves as you like. Add books from the shelf or from any book's page.

### Challenges
- Reading challenges with a prompt for each spot. Tap a spot to choose its book.

### Look and feel
- Color themes (Forest, Ocean, Plum, Rosewood, Clay, Slate), each with light and dark versions and a Match device option.
- Rosewood and Clay were recolored to be truly rosy and terracotta.
- Font choices, including an easy-reading option.

### Your data
- Syncs across every device you sign in on, and works offline.
- Import from Goodreads, StoryGraph or a Nightstand backup. Import is in the account menu.
- Download a full backup anytime.
- Installable as an app on your phone or computer.
